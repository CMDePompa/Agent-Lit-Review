# Research context for relevance assessment

The researcher studies polymer science and computational materials research, including polymer self-assembly, phase behavior, morphology, interfaces, and simulation methodology. Relevant sources may address theory, modeling, experimental characterization, or analysis methods that help interpret structure-property relationships.

Relate a source only where its actual methods or findings support a useful connection. Identify whether the connection is direct, indirect, or low relevance. Do not claim that a source studied a particular method, material system, or architecture unless the PDF establishes that.
